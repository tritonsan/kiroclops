KiroClops: what OpenCV 5 measures

Step through real recordings and see the positions, tracks and events that OpenCV 5 measured from their pixels.

OpenCV 5pixels only3 recordingsruns in the browser, no installation

Each tab is a real recording: PNG frames with their capture times. KiroClops measures every frame from the pixels alone, with no access to the DOM or the game engine. OpenCV 5 template matching finds each selected object, contours give its visible box, edge and line extraction give connector end points, and a fixed rule set turns the tracks into events such as motion start, reversal and stop. This page shows exactly what that measurement returned. Boxes and rings are measured positions, the curves are the raw x and y tracks, and each marker is an event, drawn as the interval between the two frames that bracket it. A frame without a valid measurement gets no position. Press Play or drag the slider to step through a recording.
